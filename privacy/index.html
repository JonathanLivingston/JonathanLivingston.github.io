Privacy Policy

Chick Hunt, Sewer Slash, Padeloid and Deadly Football. Last updated: 20 October 2026

This policy covers the Android versions of the games Chick Hunt, Sewer Slash, Padeloid and Deadly Football, published by Jonathan Livingston.

No data collected

The games do not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal or usage data. They have no ads, no analytics or tracking, no accounts or sign-in, and no in-app purchases. They do not request internet access and do not connect to the internet.

What stays on your device

Each game saves only its settings (such as sound and music on or off) and your scores or progress, locally on your device. For Deadly Football this is your club name, kit, crest, squad, Story progress and settings, kept in the app's private storage. This information never leaves the device. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its data in your device settings, deletes it.

Permissions

The games do not ask for any permissions to access personal information, contacts, location, camera, microphone or files.

Children

The games are not directed at children under 13. Because no data is collected, no personal information from children is collected either. Deadly Football contains cartoon violence (tackles, punches, knockouts and red cards).

iOS versions

If an iOS version of Chick Hunt or Padeloid is released, it may let you submit scores to Apple Game Center leaderboards when you are signed in to Game Center. Those scores are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. The publisher receives no personal data from this.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
